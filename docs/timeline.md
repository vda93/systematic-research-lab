# How the research decisions evolved

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [Research atlas](research-atlas.md)

This chronology describes research decisions, not the dates of the market observations. Detailed historical windows remain attached to each study.

| Research stage | Evidence that changed the next step |
| --- | --- |
| Early infrastructure and news research | Deterministic event-time alignment and state-machine comparisons separated execution defects from hypotheses. Later calendar-coverage problems showed that a mechanically valid run could use an invalid event universe. |
| Opening-range exploration | Large timing surfaces revealed local plateaus and extreme champion sensitivity. Representative selection and controls challenged exact range geometry. |
| August–September 2026: ORB validation | USTEC's first OOS block supported the frozen candidate; the next failed independently. Gold parity and execution progress did not rescue financing qualification. |
| September 25–26: MOMCONT exit research | Directional mirror, trailing ablation, earlier eras, frozen exit comparison and paired portfolio validation led back to canonical exits, with funded sensitivity indeterminate. [Study](../studies/momentum-continuation.md) |
| September 28–29: DE30 | Persistence development support was followed by concentration rejection, a separately authorized terminal read, sizing correction and bounded portfolio inclusion research. [Study](../studies/de30-continuation.md) |
| September 30: historical ORB closeout | The final gold holdout was negative or effectively flat. Tested branches closed; untested parked branches did not acquire invented failure results. [Study](../studies/opening-range.md) |
| Early October: direct feature falsification | NOWICK matched controls failed the incremental continuation claim. Later exploratory outliers did not reopen the original program. |
| October 11–12: negative mechanism findings | EBP's frozen small-candle study found a retrospective negative association; an independent NOWICK reversal contract also failed. Association, causality and opposite-direction profitability stayed separate. [Atlas](research-atlas.md) |
| October 2026: Research OS | Manifests, evidence roles, mutation detection, deterministic review packs and explicit source/checkout checks made the evidence chain inspectable. [Engineering](../studies/research-engineering.md) |
| October 20: public distillation | Completed records were surveyed across programs. Original public summaries and figures were built without reopening sealed data or modifying historical research ancestry. |

The common pattern was a narrowing of claims. Discovery could justify a validation, controls could reject a mechanism without invalidating every observation, and a completed experiment could end in a stop decision.
