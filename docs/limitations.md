# Limits of the curated public research

These case studies report selected completed private research. The public repository can verify aggregate consistency, source claims, software examples and presentational figures, but cannot independently rerun the historical market strategies without the private data, trade records and exact implementations.

Discovery, validation, previously inspected out-of-sample periods, spent holdouts, invalid economic-calendar universes and not-run experiments must remain distinguishable. A passing implementation-parity gate is not a claim of post-cost profit, broker portability or live deployment eligibility.

Matched event studies can reduce specified observational differences but do not randomize market events or prove causality. Search over correlated configurations, reused historical samples, small paired samples and model-selection effects restrict generalization. Invalid input data cannot establish an economic pass or a clean strategy failure.

R-space outcomes, price-space outcomes, monetary profit factor, commission, financing and realized brokerage execution are different objects. Numerical precision or a reproducible chart cannot recover absent fills or independently authenticate a private report.

The featured [ORBINDEX study](../studies/orbindex.md) was chosen after a large correlated development search. Its later 2024–2026 comparison carries possible prior historical exposure; the underlying custom-symbol physical template is not the full historical Exness broker contract. All fixed-lot EUR scenarios were negative in the later period, but broker-net accounting and a calibrated alpha significance claim remain unqualified. Subsequent quote-path forensics cannot retrospectively restore independent OOS status.

## Synthetic methods appendix

The [synthetic study](study.md) deliberately generates artificial concentration and inference examples. Its 80 Monte Carlo trials reflect one finite calibration experiment, not historical trading performance or a general coverage guarantee. Deterministic hashes show repeatability, not future alpha.

## Publication scope

Other historically published studies are excluded from the current curated public tree at the owner's direction. Their prior Git commits and independent private research decisions have not been rewritten.
