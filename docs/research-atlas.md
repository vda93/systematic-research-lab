# Research atlas: the wider evidence behind the portfolio

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [Evidence register](evidence.md) · [Timeline](timeline.md)

The October 2026 survey inspected the complete retrieved **114-record research registry**, then reconciled relevant primary decisions, local reports, engineering receipts and later closeouts. Related records overlap and some are protocols, source ideas or duplicate summaries; 114 is a coverage count, **not** a count of successful experiments. Local studies outside that registry were included where adequate records existed. [Census limits](evidence.md#b27)

Enough evidence to describe a result does not mean enough evidence to promote a strategy. This atlas includes completed economic tests, conditional associations, insufficient-support stops, invalid-data forensics and engineering checks. It explicitly separates unrun hypotheses. The [source register](evidence.md) explains what was inspected; private backtests are not publicly replayable.

## Source ideas, pending evidence and census limits

AMD, DRT, GLBX, SMB offside and Turtle Soup are registered source-document hypotheses with no demonstrated edge in the inspected records. VWAP H008 and Anti-EBP post-fill/post-stop proposals are parked and untested. Venom's later holdout, DE30 half-hour prospective validation and the distinct Fade successor's economic discovery remain pending in their reviewed records. They are not counted as completed economic results. [B27](evidence.md#b27)

Separate primary records for some numbered gaps, including MOMCONT H002/H020 and VWAP H002/H003, were not established in this finite survey. Missing records are a coverage limitation, not proof that no related work exists. AOF poker research is outside this trading atlas.

Source qualification and risk infrastructure were also examined as engineering context. Data correctness can enable research; it does not supply evidence of alpha. Exact live risk policies, deployment parameters, proprietary source and raw evidence bundles are deliberately excluded.

The flagships follow experiments through their decisions. The wider atlas retains the other sufficiently evidenced work, including unsuccessful and unfinished research.
