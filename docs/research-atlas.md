## ORBINDEX

I searched for opening times that worked across nearby settings, then tested the German index portfolios under a shared order limit. The later fixed-volume euro results led me to close all three portfolios. The article follows the search across Germany, Japan and the UK through the later test and its follow-up investigation. ## ORB setup and filter study

Another ORB study examines a New York cash-open breakout with candle-close confirmation and fixed stop/target geometry. The article explains the setup, the later EMA extension, 48 discovery variants, and how the same selected US500 configurations performed in sample versus later history. Its setup and results are recorded separately from ORBINDEX.
