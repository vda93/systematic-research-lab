# Research library

## ORBINDEX

I searched for opening times that held up across nearby rules on the German, Japanese and UK indices. Only the German index supplied portfolios for a later test. All three lost euros at fixed volume before additional commission or financing, so I closed them. Earlier research may have seen part of the later period.

## MacroImpulse

I tried to trade the first move after scheduled currency news. The historical baseline was promising, but across brokers the spread widened, fills came at worse prices and slippage took away too much of the move. I shelved the strategy.
