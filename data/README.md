# Public aggregate inputs and provenance

[ORBINDEX](orbindex-public.json) contains the index discovery, portfolio, later-test and follow-up summaries. The [ORBINDEX source register](../docs/evidence.md#ix1) identifies which artifacts were checked against the original research packages and which follow-up summaries came through a handoff.

[MacroImpulse](macro-public.json) contains the released news-study figures’ underlying aggregates and source identities. It includes historical cost and calendar records outside the featured article. The [figure evidence review](../docs/macro-visual-evidence.md) distinguishes reported outcomes from the fictional order illustration.

The [ORB setup and filter aggregate](orb-setup-filters-public.json) contains the 48 discovery configurations and eight later US500 cells, with source hashes, explicit missingness, and the EMA extension's untested later status. The [study](../studies/orb-setup-filters.md) defines the setup and compares the same selected configurations in sample and out of sample. Public figures include a clearly labeled setup schematic, the full discovery surface, matched gross-R-per-close comparison, every matched EMA daily effect, later commission scenarios, and a fixed-trade extra-cost curve.

The file also includes complete matched EMA summary counts, fixed-trade extra-cost sensitivities derived from the released precision, and recorded final-status counts for the primary discovery candidate's signal admission. The admission categories follow rejection precedence; the separate independent grid diagnostic overlaps other conditions and is excluded from the categorical breakdown. An apparent tie at the released precision need not be an exact tie in the private source. The [technical appendix](../studies/orb-setup-filters-appendix.md) distinguishes these publicly reproducible calculations from the reported bootstrap inference, whose daily inputs remain private.

Candlestick prices and the EMA context in the setup illustration are synthetic. They are defined in the renderer, while strategy parameters come from the reviewed rules; no market replay or performance result is implied.

The [claim registry](claim-registry.json) binds quantitative paragraphs and figures to reviewed sources. The [synthetic protocol](../experiments/protocol.json) and [synthetic results](../results/study.json) demonstrate public methods on artificial data. [Scientific checks](../docs/scientific-checks.md) explain what reproduction verifies.
